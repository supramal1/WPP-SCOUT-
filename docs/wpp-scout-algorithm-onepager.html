WPP Scout scoring approach

From messy campaign data to confident creative decisions

WPP Scout gives teams a consistent way to understand which creative ideas are working, where they are working, and what action to take next.

The visible journey

What a stakeholder needs to understand first
1

Ingest the data

Bring together Meta and TikTok campaign exports, even when the files arrive in different shapes.

2

Compare fairly

Judge each creative against the right peer group, not against unlike-for-like media buys.

3

Apply guardrails

Check confidence, fatigue, consistency, objective fit, and cost before recommending action.

4

Recommend action

Translate performance into clear next steps: scale, keep, refresh, review, or pause.

The hidden complexity

What is fed in, how it is weighted, and what protects the recommendation

What's fed in

The score uses performance metrics and campaign context, not creative opinion alone.

Media deliverySpend, reach, impressions, frequency
ResponseClicks, CTR, engagement, shares
Attention2s/3s VTR, completions, hold signals
ContextObjective, platform, buying type, format
Creative labelsConcept, placement, asset type, wave
Quality flagsConfidence, fatigue, consistency

How the score is weighted

The exact KPIs change by objective, but the weighting logic stays consistent.

50%Primary KPI
25%Secondary
15%Efficiency
10%Attention

Example: Awareness leans into attention and efficient reach; Traffic leans into CTR. Static assets use engagement and CTR instead of video completion.

Guardrails behind the answer

Only compare like with like: objective, platform, buying type, and format.
Flag low-spend or low-reach rows before calling them winners.
Reduce confidence where frequency suggests audience fatigue.
Show concept-level and placement-level views separately.

The action language

A score becomes a decision prompt
85+Top performer
70-84Scale or use as template
50-69Keep or monitor
25-49Review or refresh
<25Pause candidate

What teams get back

RankingsTop and bottom creatives by platform, objective, or concept.
ReasonsPlain-English explanations for each recommendation.
Budget signalsWhere to scale, reduce, refresh, or gather more evidence.
The point is not to automate judgement.

WPP Scout hides the analytical complexity without hiding the reasoning. Stakeholders get a simple action; teams can still trace the evidence behind it.
